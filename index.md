# Warren Buffett's Lessons Explained

### Every shareholder letter. Every lesson. 49 years of wisdom.

From 1977 to 2025, Warren Buffett wrote a letter to Berkshire Hathaway shareholders every year. These letters contain the most detailed, real-world education in business and investing ever published — for free.

This site organizes **1,091 lessons** extracted from all 49 letters into three ways to explore:
